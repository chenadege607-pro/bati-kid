## Tone

Batibo is a tonal language. A falling tone at the end of a word can
change its meaning entirely from a rising tone. When teaching, always
call out where the tone falls (e.g. "the tone falls on nnu").

## Formality

There is a distinct "elder register" used when speaking to someone
older or in a position of respect — different from the everyday
register used between peers. Elder Anyi should teach learners which
register applies in each example.

## Greetings

Greetings change based on time of day and are considered important —
skipping a greeting before a request is seen as impolite.
